---
title: "My Peace Lily is in Plant Hospital"
date: "2023-03-01"
---

Sitting here on the sofa
6am
Hoping she’ll recover.

My overwatered peace lily is in plant hospital.

It drank too much while I was away.
Far too much.

Every morning I take it to the sink. Drain the water that’s accumulated at the base.

It shocks me to see how much there is, how pale its become.

Wilted. Leaves crumbling off, often in groups.
I’ve been home for a week, but the soil is still so wet.

I discovered it all at once.
Stale water to the brim. I was unaware of what was happening to my peace lily.

Now my lily is in plant hospital.
I’m doing my best.
My lily sits in a new tray, where the water can escape.

We have a plant light in place. I turn it on as soon as I wake and my lily bathes in it’s nourishing blueness.
All day long.
While plants outside make do with February.

My lily is fighting, not fading.
No worse than when I returned.
In fact, better. Imperceptibly. Day by day.
I wonder: Is sheer hope fooling me? But it's not.

Soon there will be shoots.
My lily will grow miraculously like before.
And my lily will bear the weather, just like the others.

For now, I do all I can.
Which is to watch, and to care, and to hope, and to pray.

For my peace lily.
