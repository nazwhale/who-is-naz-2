---
title: "Good Things"
date: "2020-06-01"
---

I like how my arm hangs by my side. 
To me it's joined, it feels just right.

I like to watch my plant turn to the sun.
I like when I get out and run.

I like to lie beside the window.
To sun my legs, and air my toes.

It's good to be awake.
It's good to breathe in.
And to breathe out.

To let it in.
And slow 
right 
down.
