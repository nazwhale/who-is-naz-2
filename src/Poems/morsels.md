---
title: "Morsels"
date: "2020-06-01"
---

You're made of tropical magic
You're a psychedelic cabbage
You're a mamba
Trapped in amber
Sliding through time
In a samba.

But,
To stand, to see beyond your pond
You'd need a magic ladder
With rungs that reach beyond the sun
And even then you'd have
To stand up 
In a time you don't connect to
Find footing with those paper toes.

Still you struggle in your trances
Blind beings without glasses
Arms stretched out
Way beyond our kneeling bodies
As far as they can go

Feeling
Just sensing
Just about detecting
Some morsels of the mess we're in
Delicious morsels

Mmm
