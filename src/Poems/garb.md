---
title: "Garb"
date: "2020-06-01"
---

Many of my favourite clothes
Are on rotation

I have no clothes rotation station

Shirts on chairs
A robe over the door
Odd socks sprayed around the floor.
Hats and caps of various kinds
Several things aren't even mine.

But, despite this garb
Of which I'm fond
I'm happiest
With nothing on.
