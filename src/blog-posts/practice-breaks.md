notes from Molly Gebrian talks:

- **Interleaving** vs. big blocks
  - Practice something for ~10 mins, come back later
  - Break it up with a 2-4 min break doing antoher exercise
  - Things stick better. Too short, and brain stuff doesn't have time to happen. Too long, and the memory trace disappears.  
  - Coming back to something 2-3 times in a day for 10 mins is better than a 30 min blo k
- Increase the number of days off from it as it gets good (but do come back to it)
- **Correct repetitions** are important to solidify things
  - e.g. try to play something correctly 3x in a row, then take a 10s break. Then play 3 more in a row. If any of them mess up, start over and try to hit 9 total correct (3-break-3-break-3)
- **Isolate** difficulties
  - Spend the most time fixing the biggest issues
- If something's overwhelming, working memory is overloaded
  - Practicing technique puts that into long term memory, freeing up working memory for new things
