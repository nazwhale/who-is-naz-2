- Sincerely elevate the species
- Millions of people may engage with this thing. I have a choice to make it easy or not. 
- The user feeling that "somebody gave a shit about me"
- Come from a place of love and care when you make something. A way of expressing our gratitude to the species
- Simplicity is to succinctly express the essence of something
- Joy & humour is lacking in product right now
- How I am at this moment will be embodied in the work
- [Daniel Cook: Princess Saving talk](https://lostgarden.com/2008/10/27/the-princess-rescuing-application-slides/)
- You can't measure the output of great design. Large teams lose that in favour of easy-to-measure things.
- The idea that "what is easy to measure is what we should focus on" is a lie
- Efficiency lets you move fast. You can have both speed and breathtaking quality. 
- Etherial thoughts are fragile and precarious. So trust and care is important. 
- People desperate to speak kills ideas. Be quiet and listen. 
- Do things that result in authentic and deep relationships.
- Make things for each other. Evokes vulnerability and care and gratefullness. It can be as simple as making breakfast. Hosting in each others homes. 
- If something doesn't work, it's ugly. No matter what it looks like. 
- A sense of humanity may be more useful than beauty in guiding design decisions. 
- Users can sense care and carelessness in a product. 
- What we do when no-one sees is a powerful marker (e.g. choosing to make the back of the closet drawer beautiful)
- It's all about care. Practicing care and concern for each other is a privilege.
