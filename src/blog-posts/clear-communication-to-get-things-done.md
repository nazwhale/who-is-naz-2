In fast-moving teams, vague communication causes pain: misalignment, missed deadlines, dropped actions.

Here's my tips.

## 1. Short objective answers

Strive to reply to questions with one of:
1. Yes
2. No
3. A number
4. I don’t know (and will follow up when I do know)

### Example

**Question**: 
How many customers to we expect to have onboard by next Friday?

**Answer A**: 
Well, based on our current trajectory and taking into account the usual conversion rates we've been seeing lately, I think we can reasonably expect somewhere in the neighborhood of 150 customers, give or take a few depending on various factors. Of course, this is assuming nothing unexpected comes up with the onboarding process, which has been running pretty smoothly recently except for that one hiccup last Tuesday with the authentication service, but that's been resolved now. I can double check these numbers with Sarah from Sales if you need more precision, though she's out of office until Thursday.

**Answer B**: 
150

Not only does Answer B save everyone's time, it's also far easier for the other person to digest.

If people have further questions, instead of premeditating, let them ask.
