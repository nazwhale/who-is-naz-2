I'm 33. I'm finding that my points in conversations about kids are primative. But it's a core life task - it's worth thinking about.

Here's a dump of all the stuff in my head around the decision. 



## Thoughts/worries

> Simply what came out of my head. by no means rational / considered beliefs

- What will I lose?
- Is life over?
- Will life begin?
- What if I regret it instantly? 
- What if they're problematic / have disabilities? 
- How likely are miscarriages?
- No support network, family are far away
- Will I be able to stand the crying
- Non-stop-ness
- Losing independence / sexuality 
- Risk of divorce / seperation. And impact to kids and self
- How much of life becomes chores and what are they?
- Are teenagers a decade of horrible flatmates?
- Will no sleep make it a nightmare?



## Decision making helpers

### Byron Katie Questions

1. Is it true?
2. Can you absolutely know it’s true?
3. How do you react—what happens—when you believe that thought?
4. Who would you be without the thought?
5. Turn the thought around–what would the opposite of that thought be?



## Data

### “How likely are miscarriages?”

| Maternal age       | Miscarriage risk per pregnancy |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| 25-29 yrs (lowest) | ≈ 10 %                         |
| 30-34 yrs          | ≈ 11 %                         |
| 35-39 yrs          | ≈ 17 %                         |
| 40-44 yrs          | ≈ 33 %                         |
| 45 + yrs           | ≈ 53 %                         |

For my age,  roughly 1 in 9 pregnancies ends in miscarriage.

### “Will I cope with the crying?”

Pooled estimate across 57 studies: peak **126 ± 61 minutes** of cry + fuss per day at 5-6 weeks. [Social Policy and Administration](https://srcd.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cdev.13760?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

“Colic” (> 3 h/day) affects **17-25 %** of infants in the first 6 weeks. [EurekAlert!](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/953420)



### “Will no sleep make it a nightmare?”

- UK survey of 1,300 parents: average **3 hours’ sleep lost per night** in the first year – ≈ 133 full nights. [The Bump](https://www.thebump.com/news/nights-of-sleep-lost-in-first-year)
- Long-term panel data (Germany/UK) show measurable sleep deficits for up to **6 years**, worst at 3 months postpartum. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2019/feb/26/parenthood-sleep-deprivation-after-birth-mothers-hit-hardest-research)



### “Non-stop-ness & chores – how much time disappears?”

| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------- | ------- |
| Unpaid childcare                                             | 84 min  | 55 min  |
| Housework                                                    | 169 min | 106 min |

Office for National Statistics time-use diary, post-pandemic. [Office for National Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/articles/familiesandthelabourmarketengland/2021)



### “Will we lose our sexuality/relationship?”

- **33-50 %** of first-time parents feel dissatisfied with their sex life 6-8 months postpartum. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6732775/)
- In a study of 255 couples, sex frequency clustered at “once a week” or “2-3× week” by 6-12 months, but mothers reported **lower desire** and **higher pain scores** than partners. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6732775/)



### “Risk of divorce / separation?”

- UK survey (2023) found **≈ 20 %** of couples separate within the first year after a baby. [GoodtoKnow](https://www.goodto.com/wellbeing/relationships/relationship-news/break-up-after-baby-513710)
- Wider Marriage-Foundation analysis: by age 3, **10 %** of first-borns have seen parental breakup; by age 14 that’s **31 %**. [Marriage Foundation](https://marriagefoundation.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/MF-research-note-Source-of-family-breakdown.pdf)



### “Regret it instantly?”

- YouGov poll (2021): **8 %** of UK parents currently regret having children; another **6 %** once did but no longer. [YouGov /Research Reality](https://yougov.co.uk/society/articles/36590-one-twelve-parents-say-they-regret-having-children)



### “No family nearby – will lack of support break me?”

- Systematic review of 16 studies: **low social support is among the strongest risk factors** for postpartum depression (effect sizes similar to prior mental-health history). [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9711915/)


  

### "Are teenagers a decade of horrible flat-mates?”

- Developmental reviews show **conflict frequency and intensity rise in early adolescence but taper after ~16**, as relationships rebalance. [Social Policy and Administration](https://srcd.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cdep.12278)
- When parent relationships break down, **27 %** of UK teens show high mental-health difficulties versus **12 %** in intact families. [Institute for Family Studies](https://ifstudies.org/blog/the-link-between-family-breakdown-and-teen-mental-health-problems-in-the-uk)
