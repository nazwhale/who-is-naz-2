## Get 10 users that *really* love your product

At this stage you want to be learning really fast. 

That means creating the tightest possible feedback loop with your early users. A WhatsApp group works fine at this stage. 

Charge them something (even if it's a tiny amount). You'll get better quality feedback.

Iterate fast on feedback and [do things that don't scale](https://www.youtube.com/) to delight your users.
