- art is grown up play
- feelings matter. understanding what you actually like. when we’re being advertised and persuaded of 20000 things a day
- and why do we like it. what does it arise in us
- when we look at art, we look at it in terms of our own personal history and cultural context
- when you’re making something, it starts to come alive when you have feelings you didn’t expect from it
- when you’re working as an artist, you’re always worldbuilding 
- ambient music blurs the line between what is the music and what is the world
- dimensions in which music can exist is expanding in every dimension (short and long, quiet and loud)
- a wind chime is an example of generative music. a system from which a performance eminates. the artistic act becomes the creation of the conditions. likens to gardening, creating conditions for the seed to grow
- scenius instead of genius. the cafe owner and the art dealer played vital roles in movements
- on working with tech: try to use it in ways that the creators didn’t intend
- when looking at a 5 person band, think about all the possible duos, trios, quartets, soloists, everyones. what hasn’t been done yet with this system
