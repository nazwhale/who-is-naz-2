Delegating is all about super clear expectations. 

The clearer the expectations, the more freedom you give the person you're delegating to to confidently complete the task within those boundaries.

Here's my template.

# Template

### Task
[1-2 sentence description of what needs to be done.]

### Why it's important
[Why this task matters, how it fits into the bigger picture, who will be impacted]

### Success Criteria
[What does a successful outcome look like?]
[Any quality standards or measurable outcomes.]

### Deadline
Due: [Date + Time]

### Check-ins (optional, if task is long-running)
[Optional milestones/check-ins: If applicable]

### Resources
[Link to useful docs, templates, assets]
[People to talk to]
[Tools/access needed]

# When things go wrong

## Check-in doesn't come in

Follow up without nagging.

Some one-liners you can use: 
- “Hey, just checking in on this—any updates or blockers?”
- “Quick nudge on this one, as it’s due [date] and ties into [next step/team].”
- “Let me know if you need anything from me to get this over the line.”
- “I’m updating the project board—mind sharing where this stands?”
- “Can you give me a quick update by [time/day] so we can stay on track?”
