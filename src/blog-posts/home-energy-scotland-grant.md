***HES = Home Energy Scotland***

------

#### 30-second cheat sheet

| What you can get                   | Grant                      | Interest-free loan (optional) | Rural uplift* |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------- |
| Heat-pump or other “clean heating” | £7,500                     | + £7,500                      | + £1,500      |
| Insulation / energy-efficiency     | 75 % of cost (cap £7  500) | remainder up to £7  500       | + £1,500      |

*Applies if your address sits in the Scottish Government’s “Remote Rural/Island” band. ([Vaillant UK](https://www.vaillant.co.uk/advice/understanding-heating-technology/heat-pumps/unlock-the-benefits-of-the-home-energy-scotland-grant-and-loan-scheme/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [homeenergyscotland.org](https://www.homeenergyscotland.org/home-energy-scotland-grant-loan-terms-conditions?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Fund is **first-come, first-served** each financial year; once you have a written offer you get **9 months** to finish works and claim. ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))

### Seven-step application flow

1. **Ring HES** — 0808 808 2282 (8 am-8 pm Mon-Fri, 9-5 Sat). Ask for the *“Grant and Loan”* scheme and note the **invitation code** they email you. ([Vaillant UK](https://www.vaillant.co.uk/advice/understanding-heating-technology/heat-pumps/unlock-the-benefits-of-the-home-energy-scotland-grant-and-loan-scheme/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
2. **15-min eligibility call** — advisor runs through house type, income/benefits, and what you plan to install. They produce a **Home Energy Selector** or **Home Renewable Report**. 
3. **Get two quotes** — from MCS-certified installers (meaning they're approved to install renewable systems to government standards) for each measure (heat pump, insulation, etc.).
4. **Fill the online form** — you need the invitation code + quotes + EPC/energy report + six-year address history + (loan only) credit details. ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
5. **Wait for approval** — HES aims to process a *complete* application in **≤ 10 working days**. ([Vaillant UK](https://www.vaillant.co.uk/advice/understanding-heating-technology/heat-pumps/unlock-the-benefits-of-the-home-energy-scotland-grant-and-loan-scheme/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
6. **Install only \*after\* the written offer** — start work early and the funding is void. ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
7. **Claim + get paid** — upload invoices, MCS certificate (proof your installer and system meet government standards), and a post-installation EPC (an updated Energy Performance Certificate that shows how energy-efficient your home is after the work). The grant is paid into your bank account, and if you took a loan, repayments begin by direct debit. ([Vaillant UK](https://www.vaillant.co.uk/advice/understanding-heating-technology/heat-pumps/unlock-the-benefits-of-the-home-energy-scotland-grant-and-loan-scheme/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

### “PDF-pack” checklist (copy/paste into the blog)

| Section           | Exact info the form asks for                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Applicant details | Name, DOB, email, phone, **six-year address history**        |
| Property basics   | UPRN (Unique Property Reference Number)  or postcode, tenure (owner-occupier / private landlord), main-home tick-box. Use the free search tool at [findmyaddress.co.uk](https://www.findmyaddress.co.uk) to look up your UPRN using your address or postcode. |
| Energy data       | Latest Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) *number* (a 20-digit Report Reference Number (RRN) at the top right corner, formatted like: `1234-5678-9012-3456-7890`). If you can't find your EPC, visit the [Scottish EPC Register](https://www.scottishepcregister.org.uk/) and search using your postcode or address. **Or,** you can use a HES Selector/Renewable report reference. |
| Planned measures  | Technology type, installer name, **2 itemised quotes** inc. VAT |
| Finance bits      | Grant amount requested, loan amount, loan term, bank sort code & account for disbursement |
| Declarations      | Not started work, will use MCS (aka "certified") installers, haven’t had overlapping grant funding |

Attach (upload): both quotes + EPC/report + photo ID (passport/driving licence) + (if asking for a loan) soft-copy credit report.

### Full eligibility rules

- ✔ **You own the home** (private landlords can apply via the parallel PRS loan). ([Vaillant UK](https://www.vaillant.co.uk/advice/understanding-heating-technology/heat-pumps/unlock-the-benefits-of-the-home-energy-scotland-grant-and-loan-scheme/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
- ✔ Property is in Scotland and your **main residence**. Second homes don’t qualify.
- ✔ Measures are on the approved list (heat pumps, high-heat-retention storage heaters, most insulation types, solar PV/battery *until July 2024 cut-off*). ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
- ✔ You *haven’t* started the work.
- ✔ Installers are **MCS-certified** (or TrustMark for insulation) and provide quotes with warranty details.
- ✔ For the loan bit: you pass an **affordability + credit check**; 1.5 % admin fee (max £150) applies. ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
- ✖ No double-dipping with ECO4 (Energy Company Obligation; a UK government scheme that requires energy companies to fund home energy efficiency improvements, mainly for lower-income households), Area-Based Schemes (e.g. local council-run programmes), etc., on the *same* measure. ([fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk](https://fundingportal.energysavingtrust.org.uk/HOE01/))
